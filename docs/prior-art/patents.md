# PRIOR-ART ANALYSIS: PATENT LANDSCAPE REVIEW

## Representative Patents Reviewed

### 1. US Patent 9,842,160 — *System and method for spatio-temporal event extraction and news aggregation*
- **Applicant/Assignee**: Major Technology Enterprise
- **Scope Disclosed**: Extracting entities and coordinates from unstructured news documents to cluster events on a map interface.
- **Overlap**: Overlaps with spatial location extraction and event clustering.
- **Distinction**: Does not disclose educational exam relevance mapping or source-grounded MCQ generation pipelines.

### 2. US Patent 10,452,745 — *Automated educational question generation from textual corpora*
- **Applicant/Assignee**: Academic / EdTech Entity
- **Scope Disclosed**: Using syntactic parsing and dependency trees to form cloze and multiple-choice questions from static textbooks.
- **Overlap**: Overlaps with question generation from text.
- **Distinction**: FlashFacts operates over dynamic, heterogeneous RSS current-affairs streams with multi-level spatial and domain relevance filtering.

### 3. US Patent 11,200,281 — *Temporal knowledge graph construction for real-time news streams*
- **Applicant/Assignee**: Research Institution
- **Scope Disclosed**: Creating temporal edges between news events based on timestamp proximity and semantic similarity.
- **Overlap**: Overlaps with event relation and timeline extraction.
- **Distinction**: FlashFacts combines temporal lineage with competitive exam syllabus relevance scoring.
