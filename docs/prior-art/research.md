# PRIOR-ART ANALYSIS: ACADEMIC RESEARCH REVIEW

## Academic Literature Survey

1. **Gottschalk & Demidova (2018)** — *EventKG: A Multilingual Event-Centric Temporal Knowledge Graph*. Discloses RDF-based event knowledge graph extraction from Wikipedia and Wikidata.
2. **Rospocher et al. (2016)** — *Building Event-Centric Knowledge Graphs from News*. Demonstrates pipeline for extracting events, entities, and temporal relations from news streams using NLP pipelines.
3. **Manikandan et al. (2021)** — *Spatio-Temporal Event Extraction for Regional Current Affairs*. Analyzes local news extraction for state-level governance events.

## Synthesis & Methodological Comparison

FlashFacts builds upon established techniques in event extraction and spatio-temporal knowledge representation while establishing an integrated educational pipeline connecting event intelligence to exam preparation and grounded evaluation.
